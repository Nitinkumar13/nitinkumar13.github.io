Financial Accountability

Clear records. Responsible reporting.

We aim to maintain appropriate records for funds received and expenses incurred in connection with our activities. Financial information will be reported based on actual transactions and available supporting documentation.

  • Maintain records of relevant receipts and expenditures.
  • Keep available invoices, bills, and other supporting documents for applicable expenses.
  • Provide project updates that reflect activities actually carried out, rather than planned or estimated impact.
  • Publish relevant financial and activity information when it is available and appropriate for disclosure.
Our commitment: We will not publish invented impact figures, unverified expenditure claims, or documents that have not yet been issued.
