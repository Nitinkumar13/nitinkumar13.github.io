Clear expectations

What sponsorship means

Go Palak keeps the customer relationship simple and avoids promises that sponsorship does not create.

🐄

Farmer remains the owner

The animal remains with the participating farmer. Sponsorship does not transfer ownership.

🚫

No financial return

Sponsorship is not an investment and does not provide interest, profit share or cash returns.

🥛

No guaranteed milk entitlement

A sponsorship does not create a right to receive milk or other farm income.

📸

Care updates

Updates may include photos, videos and basic care information according to the selected plan.

📋

Animal condition can change

Animal health, availability and circumstances can change. Applicable policies will govern such situations.

🔎

Transparency first

Customers should be able to understand the animal, care partner, payment allocation and platform fee.

Important information

Go Palak is currently being developed as a privately operated commercial cow-care service platform and is not a registered NGO or charitable organisation. Sponsorship does not transfer ownership of the animal and does not provide any financial return, profit share or guaranteed milk entitlement. Any applicable Go Palak service/platform fee will be disclosed before payment. Final terms, refund policy and operating arrangements are subject to the applicable agreements and policies published by Go Palak. During development, Razorpay may be used in Test Mode; do not collect real customer payments until the live payment setup and required details are ready.
