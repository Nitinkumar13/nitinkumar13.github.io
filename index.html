Clear Expectations

What Your Care Booking Means

Go Palak keeps the service simple and avoids promises that a cow-care booking does not create.

🐄

Animal ownership does not transfer

Booking a Go Palak care plan does not transfer ownership of the animal to the customer. The animal remains with its current owner or care provider.

🚫

Not an investment

A Go Palak care plan is a commercial cow-care service. It does not provide interest, profit share, investment returns or guaranteed financial gains.

🥛

No guaranteed milk entitlement

A Phase 0 care plan does not create a right to receive milk, milk income or other farm revenue.

📸

Care updates

Depending on the selected plan, customers may receive photos, videos and basic information about the animal's care.

📋

Animal condition may change

Animal health, availability and circumstances can change. Applicable customer terms and service policies will govern such situations.

🔎

Commercial service

Go Palak is operated as a commercial cow-care service and is not presented as a registered NGO or charitable organisation.

Important Information

Go Palak is currently being developed and operated as a privately operated commercial cow-care service platform. It is not a registered NGO or charitable organisation. Customers purchase defined cow-care services; payments are not donations or investments. A care booking does not transfer ownership of an animal and does not provide any interest, profit share, financial return or guaranteed milk entitlement. Animal care, availability and updates are subject to the applicable service terms. Any Go Palak service/platform allocation will be disclosed before payment where applicable. During development, Razorpay may be used in Test Mode. Real customer payments should only be accepted after the applicable live payment setup and required account details are ready.
