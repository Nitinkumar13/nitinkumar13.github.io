Clear expectations

What Your Care Plan Includes

Go Palak keeps the customer relationship clear and avoids promises that the selected care service does not create.

🐄

Go Palak remains the owner

The animal remains with the participating farmer. The care service does not transfer ownership.

🚫

No financial return

The care service is not an investment and does not provide interest, profit share or cash returns.

🥛

No guaranteed milk entitlement

A care service does not create a right to receive milk or other farm income.

📸

Care updates

Updates may include photos, videos and basic care information according to the selected plan.

📋

Animal condition can change

Animal health, availability and circumstances can change. Applicable policies will govern such situations.

🔎

Transparency first

Customers should be able to understand the animal, care partner, selected service, payment amount and applicable platform fee.

Important information

Go Palak is a privately operated commercial cow-care service platform and is not a registered NGO or charitable organisation. A customer purchases a defined care service associated with a selected Go Palak-owned animal. The service does not transfer ownership and does not provide any financial return, profit share or guaranteed milk entitlement. Any applicable Go Palak service/platform fee, payment charges and service terms will be disclosed before payment. Phase-1 payments are currently handled through UPI with manual transaction verification. A booking is confirmed only after payment is verified. Customer Terms, Privacy Policy, Refund Policy and contact/ grievance details should be published before moving to a fully public live-payment operation.
